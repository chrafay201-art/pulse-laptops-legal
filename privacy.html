How Pulse Laptops handles your information

Effective date: July 24, 2026  ·  Lahore, Pakistan

Pulse Laptops ("we", "us") sells new and used laptops in Lahore, Pakistan, and shares product listings through Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and WhatsApp. This page explains what information we collect when you interact with us on these channels, and how we use it.

01Information we collect

We only collect information you choose to share with us, typically when you message us about a laptop:

  • Contact details — your name and phone/WhatsApp number, so we can reply about a laptop's availability, price, or condition.
  • Conversation content — messages you send us on WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, or TikTok regarding an order or inquiry.
  • Public profile information — your public name/handle on the platform you contact us from, as shown to any business account on that platform.

We do not ask for payment card numbers, passwords, or ID documents through any of these channels.

02How we use it

  • To respond to questions about laptop stock, pricing, and specifications.
  • To arrange in-person viewing, purchase, or after-sale warranty support.
  • To send you information you've asked for, such as photos or details of a specific laptop.

We do not use your information for automated decision-making, and we do not sell or rent your contact details to anyone.

03Platforms we use

Our product posts and messages are delivered through Meta's Facebook and Instagram platforms, WhatsApp Business Platform, and TikTok. Each of these platforms has its own privacy policy governing how they handle your account data as their user — we only see what you choose to send us in a conversation.

04Data retention

We keep conversation records only as long as needed to complete your inquiry or purchase, or as required for warranty support on a used unit you've bought from us. You can ask us to delete your conversation history at any time using the contact details below.

05Your choices

You can stop sharing information with us at any time by simply not messaging us further. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you've previously shared, contact us using the details below.
